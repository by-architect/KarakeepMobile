Privacy Policy for Linkstow

Effective date: September 28, 2026

Linkstow ("the app", "we") is an open-source mobile client for Karakeep, a bookmarking application. It is developed by by-architect and published under the GPL-3.0 license. Source code: https://github.com/by-architect/KarakeepMobile

Linkstow is an independent, community project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Karakeep or Localhost Labs Ltd.

Summary

We do not collect, store, sell, or share your personal data. Linkstow has no developer-run server, no accounts with us, no advertising, and no analytics. The app only talks to the Karakeep server that you choose.

Information the app handles

To work, the app handles the following information. It stays on your device or goes only to the Karakeep server you configure.

Server address. The address of the Karakeep server you enter (self-hosted or Karakeep Cloud), stored on your device.
Sign-in credentials. If you sign in with email and password, the password is sent to your Karakeep server to obtain an API key and is not stored by the app. The API key (or an API key you paste in yourself) is kept in your device's secure storage. Signing out removes it from your device.
Custom request headers. If you add headers for a proxy in front of your server (for example Cloudflare Access or Authelia), they are stored on your device and sent only to your server.
Your bookmarks, lists, and tags. The app requests this content from your Karakeep server so it can display it and let you manage it. It is not sent to us.
App settings. Preferences such as swipe actions, the "Show archived" filter, and the last opened list are stored locally on your device.
Data sent to your Karakeep server

Everything you do in the app (reading, searching, favoriting, archiving, deleting, tagging, and creating lists) is sent to the Karakeep server you configured. That server is operated by you or by the provider you chose (for example Karakeep Cloud). Its handling of your data is governed by its operator's own privacy policy, not this one.

Third-party websites

When you open a bookmark in the in-app viewer, the app loads the live web page from the website that hosts it, and link previews may load images from those sites. Those websites can see your device's IP address and other standard request information, and they have their own privacy practices. You can use Karakeep's saved Reader view or open links in your browser instead.

What we do not do
We do not collect personal data on any server we control.
We do not use advertising or tracking services.
We do not sell or share your data with third parties.
Data retention and deletion

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. To remove app data from your device, sign out in the app or uninstall it. Karakeep does not let an API key revoke itself, so if you no longer need the key, revoke it in your Karakeep web interface under Settings → API Keys.

Children's privacy

Linkstow does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same location with a new effective date.
